# Banner
  A wide banner at exactly 1500 x 500 pixels (3:1) optimized for a Twitter/X profile header — keeping in mind that the center of the 
  image is the safe zone (profile photo overlaps bottom-left, edges get cropped on mobile). Dark, near-black background with a subtle
   grain texture, like a worn blackboard. From the left edge, a faint audio waveform in soft white drifts across the frame — but as  
  it moves rightward it dissolves and re-forms into hand-drawn chalk-style sketches: a half-finished diagram, a rough circle with a  
  label, an arrow connecting two nodes, a small animated-looking card with wavy placeholder lines. The sketches have a Rough.js /
  hand-drawn quality — wobbly edges, imperfect strokes, visible personality. A few strokes glow faintly with a warm amber or soft
  blue luminance, as if just drawn moments ago. The composition feels like a freeze-frame of thought in motion — ideas caught
  mid-emergence. Negative space is generous. The word "murmur" in small, quiet, monospace lowercase sits slightly right of center to
  stay visible across all crop ratios. No tagline. The banner should feel like peering into a mind that thinks by drawing. Color
  palette: charcoal, chalk white, one accent — either warm amber (#F5A623) or muted lavender (#8B7EC8). Cinematic, contemplative, not
   corporate. Output at 1500x500.


# Logo Prompt
A minimal, hand-drawn logo mark for a product called "Murmur." The mark is a single continuous rough-sketched line that begins as a
   sound wave on the left and seamlessly transforms into a loose, gestural drawing of a geometric shape (like a half-formed cube or
  circle) on the right — as if a voice is becoming a sketch mid-air. The line has the imperfect, wobbly quality of charcoal on paper
  or chalk on a blackboard. Monochrome — off-white line on a deep near-black background (#0A0A0F). No fills, no gradients, just the
  single evolving stroke. The wordmark "murmur" sits below in a lightweight monospace typeface, letter-spaced wide, lowercase. The
  overall feeling is: something is being thought into existence. Quiet, intelligent, unfinished on purpose.
